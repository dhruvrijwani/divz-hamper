01 · The mechanics

How a custom hamper actually works

Shopify has no product type called "hamper". A custom hamper is a box product plus the item products the customer picks, kept together as one group from cart to packing slip. Shopify's nested cart lines (an add-on feature in the Cart API) let a box be the "parent" line with the chosen items attached under it. The customer sees one hamper, and the packing team sees exactly what goes in it.

"Do we need separate product templates?"

Yes. Online Store 2.0 lets each product use its own template, so we'd build four:

  • product.json: standard single item (a candle someone buys on its own).
  • product.hamper.json: curated hamper with the "customise" swap selectors and add-ons (Prototype B).
  • page.build-a-hamper.json: the step-by-step builder page (Prototype A).
  • collection.occasion.json: occasion landing pages pulling from the Occasion metaobject (Prototype C can live here).

02 · Placement

Where should customisation live?

The client asked whether this goes on the product page or the collection page. Each placement suits a different shopper, so the answer is two of them at launch and one later.

On the product page

Launch

Start from a curated hamper, then swap or add items.

  • Suits most gift buyers: they want a nice default fast
  • Highest conversion, least decision fatigue
  • Native Shopify Bundles handles up to 3 swap slots

Verdict: must-have. See Prototype B.

Dedicated builder page

Launch

Start from an empty box: occasion → box → items → card.

  • The headline feature for marketing ("Build your own")
  • Full control of steps, rules and the card
  • Custom theme code or a bundle app

Verdict: must-have. See Prototype A.

On a collection page

Phase 2

Browse the normal grid with an "Add to box" button and a hamper tray.

  • Feels like normal shopping
  • Good for large catalogues (50+ items)
  • Overkill at launch with ~20 items

Verdict: add when the range grows. See Prototype C.

Gift finder quiz

Launch

Four questions lead to a recommended hamper, which can then be customised.

  • Helps undecided buyers
  • Collects occasion data for email
  • Pure theme code using metafields

Verdict: cheap to build, strong for a new brand. See Prototype E.

07 · Australia specifics

What's different for an Australian gifting store

Wine needs a liquor licence

Blocker

Licensing is set by each state. NSW has a packaged liquor licence for online-only sellers. Victoria has a remote seller's packaged liquor licence, which needs RSA and new-entrant training and has a lead time of several weeks. Sellers must show the licence number and an under-18 notice on the site.

Victoria also limits delivery hours, and for gift orders both buyer and recipient must be 18+, with ID checked on delivery. The Hamper Emporium shows its liquor licence in its footer.

Payments for alcohol

Confirm

Shopify's help pages don't clearly say whether Shopify Payments in Australia accepts licensed alcohol sales. Some third-party sources say alcohol is restricted.

Before promising wine, ask Shopify Support with the licence in hand. Fallback: a third-party gateway, or launch alcohol-free and add wine in phase 2.

Age checks

Plan it

On non-Plus plans we can't add a date-of-birth field inside checkout (Plus only). Instead: an age confirmation in the cart, saved as a cart attribute, and the courier's ID-on-delivery service.

Chocolate in summer

Plan it

Dec–Feb heat melts chocolate in transit. A divz.heat_sensitive metafield can show a badge, add a cool-pack fee, or limit those items to metro express in summer.

Pricing & payments

Native

Show prices GST-inclusive (Shopify supports tax-inclusive pricing). Australian shoppers expect Afterpay, Apple Pay and PayPal at checkout.

Gifting calendar

Native

Mother's Day (May), EOFY corporate (June), Father's Day (Sept, Australia), Diwali (Oct/Nov), Christmas, Lunar New Year, Easter. Each one is an Occasion metaobject entry plus a homepage swap.

Gifts sent from overseas

Native

The Hamper Emporium runs pages like "send a hamper from Canada to Australia". A good fit for diaspora gifting (Diwali, Eid, Lunar New Year). Shopify Markets shows local currency while delivering in AU.

Delivery

Native

Local delivery by postcode and pickup are built in. Add couriers via an app (Australia Post, Sendle, etc.). One order ships to one address natively, so multi-recipient corporate orders need an app or CSV order import.

09 · Recommendation

What we'd pitch

A custom Online Store 2.0 theme based on Shopify's own Horizon or Dawn code. Horizon and Dawn already display nested cart lines in the cart. Start on the Grow plan (A$149/mo, or A$114/mo billed yearly).

Phase 1 · Launch

Curated + build your own

  • Curated hampers with swaps (B)
  • Build-a-hamper page (A)
  • Gift finder (E)
  • Occasion pages, delivery date, card message
  • Alcohol-free range if the licence isn't ready
Phase 2 · First season

Grow the range

  • Wine, once licensed and payments confirmed
  • Collection "Add to box" tray (C)
  • Reviews, occasion reminder emails
  • Corporate multi-recipient orders
Phase 3 · Scale

Pricing experiments

  • Fixed-price tiers via bundle app (D)
  • Subscriptions ("treat box every month")
  • Plus only if volume justifies A$3,700+/mo

First technical task

Before signing off scope: a two-day spike in a Shopify development store to prove the builder (box + 5 nested items + message) through cart, checkout, order and packing slip. Shopify's developer forum has some reports of nested cart lines misbehaving, so we test before we promise. If it fails, switch to Easy Bundles or Boxi and keep the same front-end design.
